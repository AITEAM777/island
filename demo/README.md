# Volcano-focused40-second demonstration

MP4,H.264/AAC,1280×720,24fps,exactly40seconds.

The revised movie has no visible text,subtitles,labels,logos,interface panels,mouse cursor,or voiceover. It concentrates on the actual project's volcanic effects rather than a guided UI tour.

Six camera shots show the whole diorama,crater closeup,full eruption column,close nighttime lava flows,night eruption overview,and a quiet ending. Eruption runs from second5 to33 at100% intensity; allfive slope-following lava ribbons have time to reach their full length. Day,sunset,andnight lighting reveal the emission and ballistic ejecta. The original project source,simulation and particle systems are unchanged; only the recording camera and visibility of DOM overlays are controlled in the capture helper.

The original ambient soundtrack is procedural water,wind,andvolcanic rumble created for this version,with no sampled music or speech. Browser frames use fixed1/24-second simulation steps,then FFmpeg encodes exactly960frames. No subtitle or text-rendering filters are used. The final file is checked by ffprobe and fully decoded for errors.
